# Product

A GNOME Shell extension that gives a GNOME desktop the Windows 11 taskbar and Start menu, for people who know Windows and want its layout and habits on Linux.

- Mode: Operate. Users launch, switch, and pin apps dozens of times a day; speed and familiarity beat expression.
- Visual authority: Windows 11 Fluent (pinned by the brief). Match its proportions, spacing, and states; do not reinterpret.
- Platform: GNOME Shell 50, St/Clutter, Wayland. Respect the GNOME accent color, text scaling, and dark/light preference.
